How this demo works

Disclaimer
  • Storage proofs are not run yet. The demo provider does not submit proofs of possession; real proofs can be added later.
  • Calibration network. Test tokens only. Each step takes 30–60 seconds: Filecoin produces a block every 30 seconds.
Who is who
Client
You. You own the data set and pay for compute.
Service provider (SP)
Stores your data set and runs your workloads next to it.
ComputeService
The smart contract that enforces the payment rules.
Two ways to pay
Pay for time
Rent a service next to your files, billed per epoch until you stop.
Pay per job
Order a result. The price is held and paid only if the result lands in your data set before the deadline.
What is real
Transactions
Every step is an on-chain transaction, linked to the Blockscout explorer.
Contracts
Filecoin Pay, PDP and Warm Storage (FilOz's deployment), plus ComputeService.
Execution
Your signatures, and real workloads running in containers.
